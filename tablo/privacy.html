Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026. Last reviewed: July 14, 2026.

Summary

Tablo is designed as a local-first document scanner and PDF workspace. The current version does not require an account for the core workflow, does not show third-party advertising, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data processed on your device

Tablo can process documents, scans, images, OCR text, tables, PDF files, local folders, export settings, and purchase entitlement state. This may include content that you choose to scan, import, edit, redact, export, or share.

Data collection

Dmitriy Motorin does not collect your document content through a developer-operated server in the current version. Tablo does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not use a custom backend for the core workflow.

Offline processing

The core scan, OCR, searchable PDF, table extraction, redaction, local library, and export workflows are designed to work locally on your device by default. Your documents are not uploaded to a developer-operated server for the core workflow.

Optional iCloud sync

Tablo may offer optional iCloud sync. If you enable it, document project data may be stored in your Apple iCloud account using Apple services. iCloud sync is optional, user-controlled, and off by default.

Permissions

  • Camera: to capture a document or table when you choose that action.
  • Photo library: to import an image you select.
  • Files: to import or export documents you select.

Sharing and export

When you export or share a document, the destination you select may receive the file. That app or service handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Purchases

Tablo may offer Pro features through Apple's StoreKit system. Apple handles purchase processing. Tablo reads entitlement state to unlock purchased features.

Changes

If Tablo later adds developer-operated analytics, crash reporting, cloud processing, support upload, or another remote service, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email mmotorin@gmail.com. Do not send confidential documents or unredacted personal data.
