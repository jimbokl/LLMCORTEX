Pet profiles, lab rows, source files, photos, reminders and exports stay on the device unless the owner chooses to share them.

Source-preserved import

Owners can prepare visit packets, passport proof, body weight context and questions to discuss with their veterinarian.

Not medical advice

VetCore does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, recommend medication doses, prescribe diets, triage emergencies or replace professional veterinary care. It organizes owner-held records and veterinarian handoff context.
