# AAC Autism Communicator App Store Pages

Static support, privacy, and marketing pages for the App Store Connect listing.

- Marketing URL: `https://jimbokl.github.io/LLMCORTEX/aac-communicator/`
- Privacy Policy URL: `https://jimbokl.github.io/LLMCORTEX/aac-communicator/privacy/`
- Support URL: `https://jimbokl.github.io/LLMCORTEX/aac-communicator/support/`

The copy frames the app as offline AAC communication support, visual schedules,
caregiver editing, and local privacy. It avoids diagnosis, treatment, therapy
replacement, cure, guaranteed speech outcomes, and crisis or emergency claims.
