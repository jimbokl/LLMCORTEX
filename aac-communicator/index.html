AAC Autism Communicator

iOS app for AAC card communication.

AAC Autism Communicator helps children express needs, choices, refusals, preferences and simple phrases with visual cards, a phrase strip and iOS speech output.

What It Includes

  • offline card board with large touch targets;
  • phrase strip controlled by the child;
  • visual schedule and first-then support;
  • practice mode for communication partners;
  • caregiver editing and local board customization;
  • AAC Passport export for home, school and specialist handoff.

Privacy Posture

The core communicator works offline. The app does not require an account, subscription, backend, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, camera, microphone or photo-library access before communication.
