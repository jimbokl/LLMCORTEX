Start A Session

  1. Allow camera access when iOS asks.
  2. Cover the active rear camera and nearby flash with the pad of one fingertip.
  3. Keep pressure light and the device still while Hara checks contact, pulse and stillness.
  4. When the signal is usable, follow the inhale and exhale ring.

If The Signal Does Not Become Usable

  • Try another rear lens shown by the lens selector.
  • Move the fingertip until both the active lens and flash are covered.
  • Reduce pressure if the preview is uniformly bright or the fingertip becomes uncomfortable.
  • Rest the device on a stable surface and keep the hand still.
  • Pause if the device or flash feels warm. Resume only when comfortable.

Camera Access

If access was denied, open iOS Settings, find Hara, and enable Camera. Hara cannot show the pulse signal without camera permission.

Local Data

Session records are stored locally. Removing Hara from the device removes its local app data unless the operating system retained it in a device backup.

Safety

Hara is a guided breathing wellness app, not medical care or emergency monitoring. Stop if the breathing pace, flash or device heat feels uncomfortable. If you feel unsafe or may harm yourself, contact local emergency services now.
