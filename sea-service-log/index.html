Sea time calculator and log · iPhone

Your sea time,
calculated and recorded.

Calculate days across service periods, count overlapping dates once, and subtract days not served. Then record actual service, keep evidence by vessel, and preview an unsigned CG-719S before exporting.

The calculator, journal, multi-date entry, day review, vessel folders, document imports, signer tracking, application checklists, on-device text reading, filled-form preview and complete journal backup tools are free. Unlimited PDF exports are available through one optional, non-consumable App Store purchase. There is no subscription.

Your records are stored on your iPhone. Sea Service Log has no account system, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated server. You choose when to save or share a backup or PDF.
