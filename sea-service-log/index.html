A journal for US mariners · iPhone

Your time on the water,
kept in order.

Record your vessels, dates, actual hours, roles, and waters served. Review your totals and prepare unsigned CG-719S drafts from the entries you choose.

The journal, vessel records, form readiness review, and JSON backup tools are free. Unlimited PDF exports are available through one optional, non-consumable App Store purchase. There is no subscription.

Your records are stored on your iPhone. Sea Service Log has no account system, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated server. You choose when to save or share a backup or PDF.
