Using the app

Terms of use

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sea Service Log is an independent iPhone journal for US sea service records. It helps organize information you enter and prepare unsigned CG-719S drafts.

What the app provides

The sea time calculator, local journal, multi-date entry, service-day review, vessel folders, mariner profile, document imports, signer tracking, filled-form preview and complete journal backup and restore tools are free. The app does not submit forms, certify records, provide credentialing advice, or determine whether service qualifies for a credential.

Sea Service Log is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the US Coast Guard or any government agency.

Your records and form review

You are responsible for entering accurate information and selecting the entries to include in a draft. A readiness message or completed summary is not a finding of eligibility. Journal totals and draft totals may differ when entries are excluded. Calculator estimates count calendar dates and remain separate from recorded service and credited days. Applicable credit depends on the authority, vessel, work and operating schedule.

Every newly generated CG-719S form is an unsigned draft. Saved signed attachments are labelled separately; the app does not verify their contents or signatures. Before using it, check the current official USCG form and instructions, review all filled fields and totals, complete any remaining required information, and obtain the required signatures. The app does not guarantee that a form or the service it describes will be accepted.

One-time PDF export purchase

Unlimited PDF exports are an optional non-consumable in-app purchase with a US price of US$19.99, paid once. There is no subscription or recurring app charge. The App Store confirmation shows the price and any applicable taxes for your purchase.

Apple processes the payment. Export access is enabled only after the app verifies the purchase. Cancelled, pending, unsuccessful, refunded, or revoked purchases do not provide an active export entitlement.

You can use Restore purchases in the app while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase. Restoring access requires the App Store to verify the entitlement and does not restore your journal data. See Apple’s restoration instructions.

Refund requests are handled through Apple. See Apple’s refund process. Nothing in these terms limits consumer rights that apply to you by law.

Assembled PDF packets

The export purchase covers saving and sharing generated forms, service reconciliation and indexed packets of selected forms and evidence. Filled-form and reconciliation previews are free. Imported pages in assembled packets are visual copies; digital signature validation, editable fields and interactive content are not preserved. Retain and share original signed files separately when their original properties are required. Workflow status marks reflect your entries and do not certify evidence.

Local storage and backups

Your binder, including saved documents and signer records, is stored on your device. Keep copies of important records and export a complete JSON backup before replacing, erasing, or restoring records. Restoring a backup replaces the current binder after confirmation. The separately saved calculator draft is not included in the portable journal backup. The developer cannot recover local records from an app server.

You control the files you save or share. Review them for personal information before sending them to another person or service. The privacy policy explains local storage, device backups, purchases, and support correspondence.

Availability and changes

App Store purchases and restoration depend on Apple’s services. Form requirements and instructions can change. Use the current official guidance when reviewing a draft, and contact support if the app’s output appears incorrect.

Updates to these terms will be posted here with a revised date.
