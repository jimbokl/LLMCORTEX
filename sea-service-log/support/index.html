Support

For help with Sea Service Log, email the developer at mmotorin@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. A description of the error is usually enough to start.

Keep your mariner records private. Please do not email Social Security numbers, reference numbers, signed forms, payment details, or a full journal backup. Remove names, vessel identifiers, and other personal details from screenshots. Support will not ask for your Apple Account password.

Records and backups

Sea Service Log stores your profile, vessels, and entries locally. The developer cannot view or recover them from a server. Before changing devices or erasing data, open Settings and choose Export journal backup. Save the JSON file somewhere you control.

To import a saved JSON backup, choose Restore from backup in Settings. A successful restore replaces the current journal after confirmation; it does not merge journals. Export the current journal first if you want to keep it.

If a journal cannot be read, the app preserves the original file and blocks new saves. Restore a valid backup to recover, or use the explicit erase action to start again. Do not erase the journal if you still need records that are not backed up.

Restore the PDF export purchase

The PDF export unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. In Settings or the export purchase screen, tap Restore purchases while signed into the same Apple Account used for the purchase. An internet connection is needed to contact the App Store.

A pending or unavailable purchase does not unlock exports. If the App Store is temporarily unavailable, retry later. Restoring the purchase restores export access; your service records still need their own backup. See Apple’s purchase restoration help.

Apple handles App Store billing and refund requests. If you need a refund, follow Apple’s refund instructions. Eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable law.

A form draft cannot be exported

In Forms, choose a vessel, complete your mariner and vessel details, and review every readiness message. Errors must be corrected before exporting. Warnings require your explicit review.

An entry can remain in your journal without being included in a draft: turn off Include in CG-719S draft when editing that entry. This does not make excluded service eligible, and the app’s journal totals may differ from the selected draft totals.

Before you submit a form

Sea Service Log produces unsigned drafts. Review every entry and total, complete all required remaining fields, and obtain the required signatures under the official USCG CG-719S instructions. The app does not submit forms or decide credential eligibility. Direct credentialing questions to the US Coast Guard.
