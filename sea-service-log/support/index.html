Here to help

Support

For help with Sea Service Log, email the developer at mmotorin@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. A description of the error is usually enough to start.

Keep your mariner records private. Please do not email Social Security numbers, reference numbers, signed forms, payment details, or a full journal backup. Remove names, vessel identifiers, and other personal details from screenshots. Support will not ask for your Apple Account password.

Sea time calculator

Open the calculator from Log without creating a vessel. Add sign-on and sign-off dates for up to 12 periods; both boundary dates count and overlapping dates count once. Subtract actual days not served. Your latest calculation is saved on this iPhone, separately from journal records and their portable JSON backup.

A calendar estimate does not establish credited service or credential eligibility. The expandable USCG explanation describes the conditions for a service day. Use Review days from my log to inspect actual recorded hours and shared dates.

Record several service dates

From Log, choose Add service dates, select a vessel and up to 31 actual dates, then enter the hours, role and waters once. Use another batch when these details differ. No unselected dates are filled automatically.

Application checklist

In Forms, open Application checklist and choose first OUPV, first Master, renewal or raise of grade. Each goal keeps a separate set of nine manual statuses and an optional target date. Expand an item for guidance and official NMC links. Prepared and not-applicable statuses count as reviewed; neither means the Coast Guard has accepted your documents. The planning date does not schedule a reminder.

Read document text

In Binder, open an imported document and choose Read document text. Select one page, read it locally, and use Open original to compare it. Edit the excerpt, confirm you checked it, then append it to the document notes. Keep the combined notes within 2,000 characters. The original file is unchanged, and no service days or form fields are created automatically.

Readable PDF text is used when sufficient; scanned pages and photos use text recognition on your iPhone. Recognition can misread names, dates and numbers. If a page is unclear, use a clearer original or record the needed note yourself. The text reader is free.

Records and backups

Sea Service Log stores your profile, vessels, entries, application checklists, signer records and saved documents locally. The developer cannot view or recover them from a server. Before changing devices or erasing data, open Settings and choose Export complete backup. Save the JSON file somewhere you control. It includes every saved attachment and may contain sensitive information.

To import a saved JSON backup, choose Restore from backup in Settings. A successful restore replaces the current binder after confirmation; it does not merge journals. Export the current complete backup first if you want to keep it.

If a journal cannot be read, the app preserves the original file and blocks new saves. Restore a valid backup to recover, or use the explicit erase action to start again. Do not erase the journal if you still need records that are not backed up.

Documents and signer handoff

Open Binder, choose a vessel and tap Add document. Import a readable, unencrypted PDF or single photo from Files or Photos. Each import is limited to 15 MB; PDFs can contain up to 200 pages and total saved attachments are limited to 100 MB. Photos are resized JPEG copies. Original imported PDFs can be shared from their document page without the export purchase.

Add signer details, mark the current service reviewed and record when you sent a form. These are your own workflow marks: the app sends no message and does not verify signatures. If records change after a review, handoff or signed-file import, compare the current service with the earlier files before using them.

Build a packet

In Binder, choose Build your packet and select vessels, saved documents and optional drafts or reconciliation reports. A PDF export purchase unlocks the assembled packet. Review warnings before including official-form drafts. The packet has a contents index and labelled evidence pages. Split large collections into smaller packets if you reach the page limit.

Assembled PDFs contain visual copies of selected attachment pages. They do not retain cryptographic signature validation, editable fields or interactive content. Share an original signed PDF separately when its original properties are needed. Saved signed files never replace newly generated unsigned drafts automatically.

Restore the PDF export purchase

The PDF export unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. In Settings or the export purchase screen, tap Restore purchases while signed into the same Apple Account used for the purchase. An internet connection is needed to contact the App Store.

A pending or unavailable purchase does not unlock exports. If the App Store is temporarily unavailable, retry later. Restoring the purchase restores export access; your service records still need their own backup. See Apple’s purchase restoration help.

Apple handles App Store billing and refund requests. If you need a refund, follow Apple’s refund instructions. Eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable law.

A form draft cannot be exported

In Forms, choose a vessel, complete your mariner and vessel details, and review every readiness message. Errors must be corrected before creating a draft. Open the notes and confirm your review of warnings. The filled CG-719S and reconciliation preview is free; saving or sharing generated PDFs requires the one-time export purchase.

An entry can remain in your journal without being included in a draft: turn off Include in CG-719S draft when editing that entry. This does not make excluded service eligible, and the app’s journal totals may differ from the selected draft totals.

Before you submit a form

Sea Service Log produces unsigned drafts. Review every entry and total, complete all required remaining fields, and obtain the required signatures under the official USCG CG-719S instructions. The app does not submit forms or decide credential eligibility. Direct credentialing questions to the US Coast Guard.
