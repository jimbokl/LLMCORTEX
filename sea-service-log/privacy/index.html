Privacy policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Sea Service Log stores your journal on your iPhone. Your profile, vessel details, and service entries are not sent to the developer by the app.

This policy covers the Sea Service Log iPhone app, its public support website, and support communications. The independent developer responsible for Sea Service Log can be reached at mmotorin@gmail.com.

Information you enter in the app

The app stores the mariner name and optional reference number you enter; vessel names, identifiers, dimensions, tonnage, and propulsion; and service dates, hours, roles, waters, water zones, offshore distances, notes, and draft-inclusion choices. These records are stored locally and used to display your journal, calculate summaries, and prepare drafts you request.

The app does not ask for or collect Social Security numbers. Do not put them in notes or other free-text fields. Sea Service Log does not access your location or automatically record your movements.

No app accounts, advertising, or tracking

There is no Sea Service Log account or developer-operated app server. The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not automatically send journal records to the developer or synchronize them between devices.

Backups and files you share

You can export a JSON backup and, after unlocking PDF exports, save or share a form draft using the iOS share sheet. Those files contain information from your records. You choose the destination, app, or recipient; their handling of the file is subject to their own practices. The developer does not receive a copy unless you send one directly.

Your iOS device backup, including iCloud Backup or a computer backup, may include Sea Service Log data according to your device settings. This is separate from the app’s JSON backup tool and is managed by you and Apple. See Apple’s explanation of iCloud Backup.

App Store purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time PDF export purchase. The app uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to verify whether exports are unlocked and to restore an eligible purchase. The app does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password.

Apple may process account, payment, transaction, and diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings. This policy does not replace Apple’s privacy policy.

When you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, your message, and anything you attach. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Email providers also process the message as part of delivering and storing email.

Send only what is needed to explain the problem. Do not send sensitive mariner records, Social Security numbers, signed forms, or payment credentials. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address; some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve an ongoing dispute.

This public website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or embedded third-party media. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, when serving the website, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a site governed by its own privacy policy.

Deleting and keeping records

You control how long records remain in the app. You can delete individual entries or vessels, or choose Erase all journal data in Settings to remove the stored profile, vessels, and entries. Deleting a vessel also deletes its associated entries after confirmation.

Erasing app data does not delete files you previously exported, copies held by recipients, device backups, support emails, or Apple’s purchase records. Manage those copies through their respective locations or providers. Keep your iPhone secured and save backups you need before erasing records.

Policy updates and questions

Changes will be posted here with an updated date. For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact mmotorin@gmail.com.
